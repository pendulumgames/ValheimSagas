LiteDB is intentionally pinned to 5.0.20: 5.0.21's checkpoint regression was reproduced under the installed Valheim Unity Mono runtime.

Valheim, Unity, BepInEx, Harmony, Epic Loot, Star Level System and other installed mods are inspected/referenced locally. Their binaries, decompiled source and assets are not distributed in this package. BepInEx with Harmony is a separately installed prerequisite. Consented item and character images are captured locally at runtime; standalone extracted game assets are never bundled. Public README screenshots include website views of recorded playtest media, with display names replaced. Armory fallback symbols and the web visual identity use original CSS/SVG/Unicode elements.

Seven original compatibility PNG backdrops and 24 responsive WebP copies of eight original grounded biome scenes are bundled as decorative assets. The scenes are generated artwork, not copied game assets. The delivery variants are 1920, 2560 and 3840 pixels wide; the largest is upscaled from the 2K source, not native 4K detail. Source prompts and originals are included in the source repository under assets/artwork/v034. User-supplied reference images are not distributed.
